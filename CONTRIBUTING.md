# Contributing

There are two ways a project gets listed here.

## 1. Tag your repository (automatic)

Add the topic `datafeel` to your public repository: on your repo page, click the gear next to **About**, then add `datafeel` under **Topics**.

A scheduled job rebuilds this list every Monday. Tagged repositories appear under "More projects tagged `datafeel`" with their description, stars, and last update date.

## 2. Submit a full entry (curated)

Featured entries include the team, event, track, description, and demo video. To get one:

- [Open a project submission](../../issues/new?template=submit-project.yml), or
- Open a pull request that adds your project to `projects.yml`. Follow the field notes at the top of that file.

Only list teammates who agreed to appear publicly.

## Keep your repo easy to use

Projects that others can learn from tend to have:

- A README with a photo or GIF, a one-paragraph summary, and setup steps
- A note on how the DataFeel Dots are wired and which SDK you used
- An open-source license (MIT is the simplest). Without a license, others can view your code but legally can't reuse it.

## Removal

To remove or update your project, open an issue or remove the `datafeel` topic from your repository.
